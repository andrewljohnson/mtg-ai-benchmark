03 / THE RULES OF THE EXPERIMENT

A result you can interrogate.

01

Control the matchup

Each selected pilot plays every ordered deck pairing against each other selected pilot. The same seed is played twice, with pilots and their decks swapping seats. Games use Penta's one-conclusion mode, without sideboarding.

02

Respect hidden information

Pilots see only their own redacted observation, legal actions, and a reference for visible cards. No seed, opponent hand, shuffled library, or unredacted game state enters the decision prompt. Unique legal moves advance automatically.

03

Keep the receipts

Public run manifests pin the Penta commit, simulation fingerprint, prompt, reasoning effort, decks, and limits. Compressed journals retain observations and submitted actions. Model calls are capped; errors and cutoffs are reported separately.

04

Show the uncertainty

Win rate means wins divided by all completed games, including draws. Wilson 95% intervals describe observed strict wins; paired seeds introduce dependence, so these are descriptive, not paired significance tests. Unfinished games never become losses or draws.

What this measures

Decision quality in the supported Penta simulation, not mastery of every Magic rule. Engine coverage varies by format and card. Subscription pilots, command-line pilots, and future API pilots are distinct conditions and should be compared in matched runs. This project is independent of Wizards of the Coast and OpenAI.
